About this preview

A working visual preview of the platform proposed to HYBEC under quotation ED-Q-0006 (20 Sep 2026). Every module in the approved scope is represented here with sample data:

  • ✓Sales process automation — leads with scheduled follow-ups (Google Calendar sync), templated enquiries, quotations with revision history, orders, DMR field tracking with GPS, travel claims.
  • ✓Purchase & vendors — indents, vendor allocation, purchase orders, debit notes, vendor advance wallets.
  • ✓Stock, warehouse & dispatch — species/size/grade SKUs, multi-level pricing, stock blocking with auto-release, transfers between yards, consignment stock, barcodes, gate passes, transport & e-way bills.
  • ✓Customers — credit control, aging, collection automation, advance wallets and support ticketing.
  • ✓Accounting, GST & assets — approval-driven vouchers, day book, cashflow planner, tax profiles & extra charges (cutting/planing), GST returns auto-prepared with e-invoice and e-way bill, barcoded asset register.
  • ✓HR, payroll & administration — attendance, leave, performance evaluation sheets, payroll runs, email templates with dynamic fields & digital signatures, multi-level roles and full activity logging.

The platform is custom-built for HYBEC and delivered as HYBEC’s own — all 35 users included, with no per-user licence fee and no recurring licence/AMC lock-in.

Try it: switch the role at the top right to see permission-based access change live, open Approvals and action a request, or click a quotation to see its revision trail.
